Wedding Photo Personalizer

Upload your invitation photo, choose where the recipient name should appear, then create one personalized image per recipient — all in your browser.

1. Your photo and names

Upload a JPG, PNG or WebP image. The original photo dimensions are preserved.
Use multiple lines for one invitation. Leave a blank line between recipients to create separate images.
Amita Regular and Bold are built into this single HTML file. Hindi text is rendered using the browser's text shaping.
Upload a photo to begin.
